Frequently asked

Things people ask before signing.

Is this financial product advice?
No. Baobab provides planning, modelling and tax-strategy work — not personal financial product advice and not investment recommendations. We will tell you the shape of a year and the tax position behind it. We won’t tell you which fund to invest in or which super to switch to. Where personal advice is needed, we work alongside an Authorised Representative under our AFSL.
Do you also do my tax return?
We don’t lodge returns directly. The portal produces a tax estimator that your accountant (or our partner accounting practice, separately engaged) uses to lodge. The estimator is built around the same FY rates and offsets your accountant uses, so the numbers reconcile.
What happens if my circumstances change mid-year?
You update the portal. New job, new property, new baby — you can edit any cell at any time and the calcs flow through. Material changes (a sale, a redundancy, a separation) trigger an out-of-cycle review with your planner at no extra charge.
Can I cancel?
Yes — engagements run twelve months and renew annually. Cancel at any time and pay only for the quarter in progress. Your data is yours; export it any time.
Do you take commissions?
No. We charge a flat annual fee. We do not take trail commissions, asset-based fees, kickbacks from product providers or referral fees from accountants or lenders. The fee covers the work.
Who reviews my numbers?
A qualified financial planner (FAS-registered, AFSL authorised representative) — not a junior, not a chatbot. On Bespoke engagements, the partner who onboarded you stays through the year.
