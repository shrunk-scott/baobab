Methodology

A workbook is a habit. A planner is the discipline.

Baobab’s method is forty-two pages of accountant’s craft compressed into a portal you actually open. Below: the principles that shape it, the four-stage process, and what each quarterly review covers.

01 / Principle

One workbook, not seven tabs in seven apps.

Tax, cashflow, debt, super, properties — they only make sense linked. Change a trust distribution and watch the tax balance, the savings rate and the goal timeline all move at once.

02 / Principle

Plan in February. Not at year-end.

By the time the BAS is in, the year is locked. We model your liabilities monthly, with quarterly nudges from your planner, so adjustments happen while they still matter.

03 / Principle

The numbers are yours.

Your data lives in your browser, on your device. We see what you share in our quarterly working sessions. No background sync, no third-party data brokers, no Plaid pipe.

The four stages

From first call to your fourth-quarter check-in.

01
Week 0–2

Discovery & statement of position

A 90-minute call, then a structured questionnaire. We capture income, entities, assets and liabilities, and your goals — the narrative ones first, the numbers second.

  • Income from every entity — salary, trust, business, rental, dividends
  • Statement of A&L — properties, super, shares, cash, all debt
  • Goals: education, retirement, the next investment, lifestyle targets
  • Tax history — last three returns, current PAYG instalments
02
Week 2–4

Build the plan

Your planner builds the workbook. Twelve schedules, populated with your numbers, the FY rates, the assumptions you signed off on. We deliver a written plan and the live portal at the same time.

  • Tax estimator with FY26 brackets, LITO, Medicare and franking
  • Twelve-month cashflow calendar with seasonal mapping
  • Debt schedule with interest split, deductibility flags, repayment paths
  • Super projection to retirement; goal progression with deadlines
03
Day 30 onwards

Live portal & auto-save

You get the dashboard. Type your latest balance, drop in a new bonus, change a rental tenant — every number recalculates and persists to your browser. We don’t need to rebuild anything when you make a change.

  • Every cell editable, every calc transparent
  • Auto-save to your device — no third-party storage
  • Export-to-JSON for advisers, accountants, lenders
  • Tweakable theme, density and palette to match how you read
04
Quarterly · 4× a year

Reviews with your planner

Four scheduled sessions a year. Each tied to a moment in the financial calendar. A planner walks the workbook with you, flags drift, and writes the next quarter’s actions.

  • Q1 — September: Tax position update, BAS prep, super contributions strategy
  • Q2 — December: EOFY pre-mortem, trust distribution decisions, capital gains harvesting
  • Q3 — March: Tax return wrap, cashflow refresh, goal pacing
  • Q4 — June: New FY plan, fresh assumptions, refreshed targets

The data promise

Your numbers stay in your browser.
Always.

Local-first storage

Everything you enter is saved to your browser via the standard Web Storage API. We don’t own a copy. We don’t back it up to a server. Clear your browser, it’s gone.

You bring data to us

Quarterly reviews are working sessions — you walk us through the portal on screen. We take notes. Anything we hold for the engagement is on encrypted disk under our AFSL retention obligations.

Export, on demand

One click sends your full state to a JSON file you control. Hand it to an accountant, a lender, a different planner. We won’t hold onto your numbers as a moat.
